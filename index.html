Where we're eating

Food & dining

A Seaport lunch crawl, an outdoor raw bar, coffee and ice cream, and pizza in Cambridge.

🦞

James Hook & Co

Working lobster company on Fort Point Channel since 1925 — lobster rolls, chowder, right on the water. Opens 10 AM, first stop of the day.

🦪

Island Creek Raw Bar

Island Creek's seasonal outdoor raw bar in the Seaport (99 Autumn Lane) — oysters, clam chowder, lobster bao, fish & chips. First come, first served. Opens 12 PM.

☕

Ralph's Coffee

Ralph Lauren's first permanent Boston cafe — retro green espresso bar at 70 Pier 4 Blvd. Open 7 AM – 5 PM. Amanda's pick.

🍦

Salt & Straw

Portland's cult ice cream shop, now at 200 Seaport Blvd — small-batch, weird-in-a-good-way flavors. Right in the mix with the market and Ralph's.

🍕

Si Cara

Canotto-style pizza + natural wine in Central Square, Cambridge (425 Mass Ave). Dinner ~5:30 PM — or grab takeout if everyone's wiped.

🔥

Sunday Park BBQ

After church at Arsenal Community Park, Watertown. Grill out in the park before Sophia & Shawn drive home.
